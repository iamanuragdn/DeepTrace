    How it works

    Our advanced AI models analyze and trace media to determine authenticity.

    1. Upload & Process

    The user uploads an image or video, which is securely temporarily held for analysis.

    2. Deep Pattern Analysis

    Our underlying PyTorch models analyze pixel inconsistencies, noise patterns, and face manipulation markers to generate a probability score.

    3. Origin Tracing

    We cross-reference the media across social platforms to find the earliest upload date and source.

    Restoring Trust in Digital Media.

    With the rapid rise of AI-generated content and deepfakes, discerning what is real has never been more difficult. DeepTrace was built on the necessity of tracing digital origins to fight misinformation, providing clarity and confidence in an era of synthetic media.

    The Technology

    Custom AI Models

    Trained on vast datasets of both synthetic and authentic media, our PyTorch-based detection models analyze subtle pixel-level anomalies and noise patterns.

    Real-time Web Tracing

    Our robust web scraping and indexing infrastructure cross-references media footprints across major social networks to pinpoint the earliest upload source.
